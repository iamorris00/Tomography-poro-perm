# Baseline: Chery E5 Luxury vs. Colombian EV SUV Market (as of 2026-09-26)

## Important naming note / uncertainty flag

In Colombia the vehicle is sold and marketed simply as **"Chery E5"** (not "Omoda E5"), in two trims: **Comfort** and **Luxury**. It is the same car sold elsewhere as the Omoda E5 (also called Omoda 5 EV in Spain/Chile). Technical specs (battery kWh, WLTP range, DC charge kW) below are sourced mostly from Spanish/Chilean Omoda 5 EV/E5 technical sheets because Colombia-specific spec pages could not be fetched directly (site blocked by network egress in this session — `elcarrocolombiano.com` returned EGRESS_BLOCKED). Colombian press cites slightly different range numbers (422 km, one source) — **treat the exact WLTP figure as approximate/uncertain (~410-430 km) until confirmed on the Colombian Chery site**. Prices below are the ones reported in Colombian media/search results in 2026; always re-verify on the brand's official Colombia site or with a dealer before purchase, as these prices move often ("efecto Tesla" price war noted below).

## 3. Brand perception / resale value / Bogotá dealer network (qualitative, based on general market reporting found)

- **Tesla**: Newest entrant (2026) but became the best-selling car in Colombia by April 2026, suggesting strong brand pull and pricing competitiveness; dealer/service network in Bogotá is new and still building out compared to legacy brands — a risk factor for service/parts availability short-term.
- **Kia and Hyundai**: Long-established, large dealer networks across Bogotá and Colombia, strong resale value historically (both brands have decades of presence with ICE vehicles), and their EV lines (EV3/EV5, Kona/Ioniq5) carry that trusted service infrastructure — generally considered safer bets for resale/service than newer Chinese entrants.
- **Chevrolet**: Very large, long-standing dealer network in Colombia (GM Colmotores has manufactured/sold Chevrolets domestically for decades) — strong service infrastructure, though its EV push (Equinox EV/Blazer EV) is very recent and resale data for these specific EV models doesn't yet exist.
- **Volvo**: Premium brand, smaller dealer network than Kia/Hyundai/Chevrolet but strong safety/premium brand perception; EX30 priced surprisingly close to Chinese EVs, giving good perceived value.
- **BYD**: Rapidly growing dealer footprint in Colombia (BYD Auto Colombia), aggressive on price and tech; brand perception has improved quickly given global BYD scale, but resale value track record in Colombia is still young (few years of data) compared to Kia/Hyundai/Chevrolet.
- **Chery/Omoda (Chery E5)**: Newer to the EV space in Colombia; Autovardí is the distributor. Brand recognition for Chery exists in Colombia via ICE models (Tiggo line) for years, giving it a more established service base than some newer Chinese brands (GAC, Deepal, Zeekr, Jetour), but resale value for Chery EVs specifically is unproven.
- **GAC, Deepal (Changan), Zeekr, Jetour/Jaecoo**: All very recent entrants to Colombia (2025-2026 launches per sources found), with limited dealer networks concentrated mainly in Bogotá/major cities and no meaningful resale track record yet. Treat resale value as **unknown/high-risk** until the market matures.
