Date of research: 2026-09-26
Baseline: NEW Chery/Omoda E5 Luxury ≈ COP 120–140M (360° cam, ADAS, pano roof, wireless CarPlay, V2L)

## IMPORTANT ACCESS NOTE
Direct page fetches (WebFetch) to **all** TuCarro/MercadoLibre subdomains
(`carros.tucarro.com.co`, `listado.tucarro.com.co`, `articulo.tucarro.com.co`,
`vehiculos.tucarro.com.co`) were **blocked by the network egress proxy**
(`EGRESS_BLOCKED`) for every attempt made. No listing page could be opened
directly. All data below comes from **Google/web-search result snippets**
(WebSearch tool) that surfaced TuCarro category pages and individual
`articulo.tucarro.com.co` item titles/prices in the search index. These are
marked **[snippet-only]** — none were verified by opening the actual page, so
mileage/features/exact trim details are only as complete as what the snippet
exposed. Treat every row as needing a manual click-through confirmation
before purchase. Prices, km and "features" text are quoted verbatim from
what the snippets stated; anything not stated is "unknown".

- Top used-EV brands by volume in Bogotá: BYD (180), BMW (138), Volvo (85), Mini (62), Kia (59).
- Popular models by listing count: EX30 (52), EV5 (48), Cooper SE (38), iX3 (35), i3 (33).
