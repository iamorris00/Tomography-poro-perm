### Parity checklist (14 items used for scoring)
1. 360°/540° camera (mandatory) 2. Full ADAS (adaptive cruise+blind spot+cross-traffic+AEB+lane-keep)
3. Sunroof/panoramic roof 4. LED headlights 5. Power front seats 6. Heated seats
7. Ventilated/massage seats 8. Leather/eco-leather upholstery 9. Large touchscreen (≥10")
10. Wireless CarPlay/Android Auto 11. Wireless phone charger 12. Dual-zone climate
13. Keyless entry/push start 14. V2L (bonus)
SUV/crossover body is a **pass/fail gate**, not scored.
